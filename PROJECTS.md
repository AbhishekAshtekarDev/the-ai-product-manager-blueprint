# Five AI PM portfolio projects that replace a degree

Part VIII of **[The AI Product Manager Blueprint](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/)** walks through five projects end to end. Each one is scoped like a real product, with a target user, a problem, success metrics, evaluations and risks, so it works as proof in an interview.

## 1. AI Interview Coach

Chapter 47. Role-aware interview practice with structured feedback. It shows personalisation, progress tracking, evaluation and trust design in a problem every job seeker already understands.

## 2. Voice of Customer Co-Pilot

Chapter 48. Turns reviews, tickets, interview notes and survey answers into grouped pain points with the source evidence attached. It shows how AI supports product judgment without replacing it.

## 3. Rebuilding a Real Product Feature With AI

Chapter 49. Pick one painful workflow in a real product and redesign it. The test is judgment: AI earns its place only where rules and interface changes fall short.

## 4. Meeting-to-Execution Co-Pilot

Chapter 50. Turns a meeting into decisions, owners, deadlines, open questions and a follow-up draft. It closes the gap between discussion and follow-through.

## 5. RAG-Based Knowledge Base Assistant

Chapter 51. The common enterprise AI pattern. It retrieves the right passages and answers with citations. It respects permissions and refuses honestly when the sources run out, and you measure it with evals.

How to present them is in Chapters 52 and 53 of the book, and in the free [AI PM portfolio projects guide](https://theaiproductmanagerblueprint.com/blog/ai-product-manager-portfolio-projects/).
